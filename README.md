# VITY EAEU Gateway

Create a premium, professional, modern corporate website for 5D Group, a pharmaceutical and dietary supplement distribution company operating across the Eurasian Economic Union (EAEU).

The website must position 5D Group as a serious, reliable pharmaceutical and nutraceutical company and clearly communicate that 5D Group is the exclusive representative and distributor of the British brand VITY Vitamins & Supplements throughout the EAEU market.

The website should NOT look like a generic online store. It should look like a professional pharmaceutical / healthcare / nutraceutical corporate website, with strong credibility, elegant product presentation, clear corporate identity, and a premium but calm visual style.  1. COMPANY POSITIONING

Company name:

5D GROUP

Main business:

Pharmaceutical and dietary supplement distribution

B2B distribution

EAEU market development

Exclusive representation of VITY Vitamins & Supplements

Cooperation with pharmacy chains, distributors, marketplaces and other professional partners

Main geographic market:

EAEU — Eurasian Economic Union

The website should clearly communicate that 5D Group represents the VITY brand exclusively across the EAEU market.

Suggested main positioning:

5D GROUP
Pharmaceutical & Nutraceutical Distribution

Supporting statement:

Exclusive VITY Vitamins & Supplements partner across the EAEU

Alternative supporting message:

Bringing trusted British-quality supplements to the EAEU market

Do not overuse marketing slogans. The tone must remain professional, corporate and credible. 2. DESIGN DIRECTION

Create a sophisticated pharmaceutical / nutraceutical visual identity.

The design should be:

Professional

Clean

Calm

Premium

Modern

Trustworthy

International

Pharmaceutical / healthcare inspired

Attractive without being flashy

Avoid:

Dark corporate consulting aesthetics

Excessive gradients

Neon colors

Aggressive sales design

Overcrowded layouts

Cheap-looking ecommerce styling

Excessive animations

Use a light, elegant color palette.

Suggested colors:

White / warm white background

Very light blue

Soft pharmaceutical blue

Deep navy blue for important headings

Soft green / teal accents

Very subtle gray sections

The overall feeling should be:

British pharmaceutical quality + modern European healthcare design + international B2B company.

Use generous white space, elegant typography, subtle shadows and rounded but professional cards.  3. WEBSITE STRUCTURE

Create the following main pages:

Home

About 5D Group

VITY — Exclusive Brand

Products

Product Categories

B2B / Partnership

EAEU Distribution

Contact

Navigation should be simple and premium.

Header:

5D GROUP logo

Home

About

VITY

Products

EAEU Distribution

B2B Partnership

Contact

Language selector

Use a sticky header with subtle background transparency when scrolling. 4. HOMEPAGE

Create a strong hero section.

Hero should visually communicate:

5D GROUP
Pharmaceutical & Nutraceutical Distribution

Headline:

Connecting Trusted Supplement Brands with the EAEU Market

Subheadline:

Exclusive EAEU representation and distribution of VITY Vitamins & Supplements from the United Kingdom.

Primary CTA:

Explore VITY Products

Secondary CTA:

Become a Partner

Hero visual:

Use high-quality images of VITY supplement products from the supplied catalog.

The products should be displayed elegantly, not as a chaotic collage.

Use a premium pharmaceutical environment:

Clean laboratory / healthcare inspired background

Soft lighting

White and light blue environment

Product bottles/packages clearly visible

Subtle botanical / scientific visual elements if appropriate 5. COMPANY INTRODUCTION

Create an About section immediately after the hero.

Title:

5D Group

Text:

5D Group is a pharmaceutical and nutraceutical distribution company focused on bringing high-quality dietary supplements to the EAEU market.

The company develops B2B partnerships with pharmaceutical distributors, pharmacy chains, marketplaces and other professional partners.

5D Group is the exclusive representative of the British VITY Vitamins & Supplements brand throughout the EAEU.

Create 3 or 4 professional statistics/cards:

EAEU Market
Distribution across the Eurasian Economic Union

Exclusive Brand
VITY Vitamins & Supplements

B2B Focus
Professional distribution and partnerships

Quality Focus
International quality standards

Do not invent numerical statistics such as years of experience, number of partners or number of countries unless they are provided later. 6. VITY BRAND SECTION

Create a visually strong dedicated section:

VITY Vitamins & Supplements

Subtitle:

British Vitamins & Supplements

Use the VITY catalog information.

The supplied catalog describes VITY as a UK vitamins and supplements brand and presents standards including:

GMP

MHRA

BRC

ISO 9001:2008

EFSA

FSA

EAC

Present these carefully as the standards / compliance framework stated in the official B2B catalog.

Create a premium "Quality & Standards" section with clean icons.

Important:

Do not invent certifications that are not explicitly confirmed.

7. VITY EXCLUSIVITY Create a dedicated section:

Exclusive VITY Representation in the EAEU

Text:

5D Group is the exclusive representative and distribution partner for VITY Vitamins & Supplements throughout the Eurasian Economic Union.

Explain that this partnership enables professional distribution of VITY products through the EAEU market.

Use a visual map of the EAEU region.

The map should visually highlight:

Armenia

Belarus

Kazakhstan

Kyrgyzstan

Russia

Do not make the map overly political. It should function simply as a business/geographical distribution visualization.

8. PRODUCTS SECTION

This is one of the most important sections of the website.

Create a premium product catalog using the actual VITY products from the supplied PDF.

DO NOT invent products.

Extract the available product information from the catalog and create individual product cards.

Every product card should contain:

Product image from the supplied catalog

Product name

Product category

Dosage / formulation information where available

Main ingredients / active components where available

Intended purpose where stated

Product code where available

CTA: "Product Details"

Use a clean pharmaceutical product-card layout.

Do NOT create an ecommerce shopping cart unless explicitly requested later.

This is a B2B corporate product catalog, not an online retail store.
