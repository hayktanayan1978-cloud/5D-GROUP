# 5D Group Corporate Website

## Goal
Build a polished, responsive corporate website positioning 5D Group as the exclusive VITY Vitamins & Supplements representative and distributor across the EAEU.

## Pages and shared structure
- Create a premium sticky header, mobile navigation, language selector, and light corporate footer shared across the site.
- Build dedicated pages for Home, About, VITY, Products, Product Categories, B2B Partnership, EAEU Distribution, Contact, and individual product details.
- Give every page unique search and social metadata.

## Content and experience
- Use the supplied copy and product data exactly; avoid unsupported claims, certifications, statistics, and invented products.
- Build a reusable product catalogue with search and filters for category, type, format, and ingredient.
- Add individual product pages with formulation, code, intended positioning, features, and B2B inquiry actions.
- Add EAEU distribution visuals highlighting Armenia, Belarus, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, and Russia without political framing.
- Add a professional contact form and partner inquiry experience without pretending submissions are stored or emailed.
- Structure copy for later English, Russian, and Armenian expansion; English will be active now and unavailable languages clearly marked as upcoming.

## Visual direction
- Use warm white, pharmaceutical blue, deep navy, soft teal, and restrained gray through semantic design tokens.
- Apply elegant typography, generous spacing, subtle borders and shadows, restrained motion, and accessible responsive layouts.
- Keep the experience corporate and editorial rather than ecommerce-focused; no cart, pricing, or aggressive sales patterns.

## Product imagery constraint
- The uploaded file contains product information but no embedded product images or PDF pages. Use clearly neutral, branded product-image placeholders rather than inventing VITY packaging, with the layout ready to swap in approved catalog imagery when supplied.

## Technical details
- Centralize product and category data in reusable typed modules.
- Create shared layout, product, map, and inquiry components.
- Use TanStack routes for all major sections and product detail URLs.
- Verify desktop and mobile views, filters, navigation, product detail links, and form states in the running preview.
